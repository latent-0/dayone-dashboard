Dayone Venture Partners · Lead gen funnel · United States + Europe Dayone Ventures

Sponsors buy proof, so lead with a Value Map. Reach, capture, convert.

Goal: a steady flow of qualified sponsor and CEO briefings for Dayone’s operating-partner model. One repeatable system: named-account outbound, LinkedIn-led content, and a single capture path that ends in a scoped diagnostic. Every stage shares one voice and one next step.

Who and where

Buyers: sponsors (partner, principal, operating partner), portfolio CEO / CFO, and founders of lower-middle-market software, about $5M–$40M ARR (€5M–€40M).

United States · core (suggested ~60%)

New York, Boston, Chicago, Texas, SF Bay, Atlanta, Philadelphia / NJ. Reach 8–10am local, Tue–Thu.

Europe · expand (suggested ~40%)

UK & Ireland, DACH, Benelux, Nordics, France. B2B outreach on legitimate interest, one-click opt-out.

Excluded: India and Nigeria. Post window that reaches both: 13:00–15:00 CET (7–9am ET).

TOFU · Reach

Where is the value leaking?

Be the first useful thing a sponsor reads about operating partners.

Assets
  • Partner-led LinkedIn. 3 posts a week from 3 partner profiles plus the company page: six dimensions, framework, operator POV.
  • Named outbound. 20 accounts a week, 2–3 contacts each, 5 emails + 3 LinkedIn touches over 21 days.
  • Answer pages. “Operating partner vs consultant”, “PE value creation for software”, so AI assistants can cite Dayone.
  • Podcasts and communities. One PE or SaaS podcast a month; 10 target-account comments a day.
Channels

LinkedIn, email, search and answer engines, sponsor and mid-market events in both regions, introductions from portfolio executives.

Measure

ICP profile views and followers, positive-reply rate, non-brand impressions, AI citation check (10 prompts weekly). One metric to beat: positive replies per week.

MOFU · Capture

One page, three doors

Turn a reader into a named, qualified contact who raised their hand.

Assets
  • Operating-partner landing page. Value Map sample, scorecard, book a briefing. Org, role and country on every form.
  • Comment keyword “MAP”. Auto-DM with the sample on LinkedIn posts.
  • Monthly Operating Library note. One lesson, to the captured list and as a LinkedIn newsletter.
  • 4-email nurture. Days 0, 3, 7, 14: one case, one framework, one outcome, one invitation.
Channels

Link in first comment (not the post body), bio link, outbound email, newsletter, podcast show notes.

Measure

Captures per 100 visits, ICP share (in region, in segment), scorecard completions, briefings booked. Live in this dashboard from the form sheet.

BOFU · Convert

Diagnostic before capital

A first scoped diagnostic inside the quarter, then a mandate across the hold.

Assets
  • Sponsor briefing. Walk one live company through the four phases, under NDA, with a one-page next step.
  • Scoped diagnostic. One company, six dimensions, a Value Map in a fixed window. “No capital moves ahead of the diagnosis.”
  • Proof stack. Framework, the captive technology group and the 100+ platforms it has delivered, sponsor references with consent.
  • Mandate proposal. Scope, fees, and the numbers Dayone is accountable for.
Expansion loop

After a diagnostic, propose a second portfolio company and ask for two introductions.

Measure

Capture to briefing, briefing to diagnostic, diagnostic to mandate, cycle length in days, declared pipeline.
